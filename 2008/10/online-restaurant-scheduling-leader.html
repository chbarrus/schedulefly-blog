Online Restaurant Scheduling Leader Schedulefly Appoints Wil Brawley as EVP

Thursday, October 23, 2008
We are proud to announce the addition of Wil Brawley as our EVP - focusing on sales, business development and the customer experience. Wil brings over a decade of client-centered executive experience to the team and is expected to help build on the strong industry momentum Schedulefly has garnered in 2008.

“Restaurant Scheduling is undergoing a transformative shift from excel and paper to the web. I am excited to work with Schedulefly’s hundreds of current clients and the tens of thousands of restaurants out there who are just awakening to the operational efficiencies and ease of use of web-based tools like Schedulefly,” notes Brawley.

We at Schedulefly are honored Wil has chosen to lead our business development and customer experience teams – we know our clients will reap the rewards from his decision.
