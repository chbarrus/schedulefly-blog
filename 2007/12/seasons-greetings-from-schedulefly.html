Seasons Greetings from Schedulefly!

Wednesday, December 12, 2007
Hello out there all you crazy Schedulefly users - we wanted to take this opportunity to thank you all for making 2007 a great debut year for Schedulefly. We hope you are enjoying online scheduling with Schedulefly and it is making your life a little bit easier. We have been working hard to improve and enhance the Schedulefly experience for you this year - including, in just the last month or so:

- A simpler and more intuitive Scheduling interface
- Facebook integration that allows you to view your work schedule from your profile page
- Fun picture upload feature so you can 'see' who you are trading schedules with (keep it clean!)

Look for more enhancements in the next few weeks and throughout all of 2008. A lot of our ideas come from you guys – so don't be shy about contacting us and letting us know how we can better serve you!

Well, we just wanted to say "Thanks" and to wish you all a very festive Holiday Season - Happy Holidays!
