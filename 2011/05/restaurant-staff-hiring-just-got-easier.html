Restaurant staff hiring just got easier and more fun!

Monday, May 16, 2011
I wanted to point out some helpful additions we've made to our simple job post tool, well, it's more of a job sharing tool. Our customers create a simple job opening page and then share a link to the page with their staff, with their friends and their social networks - or anywhere they want. When people apply for the jobs, we store the applicant's information inside Schedulefly for managers to review from anywhere. They also get an email alert each time someone applies with their contact information.

So onto our new additions. We have added a way for the applicants to upload a photo of themselves along with their contact information, comments and resume. A list of dozens (or more) of applicants is a little less overwhelming when you can see photos of the people looking to join your team. Also, we added a way for managers to leave comments on each applicant. This is a nice way for hiring managers to leave notes for themselves and other managers while they are considering who they are going to hire. This is also helpful when a job that has been filled comes open again. Managers can review notes left on applicants from the first round of hiring.
