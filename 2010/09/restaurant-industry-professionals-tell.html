Restaurant Industry Professionals Tell Us Why They Love Schedulefly

Friday, September 3, 2010
Yesterday we stuck a note in the Schedulefly app asking people for a minute of their time to tell us how we are making their lives easier. It's really helpful for us to share comments like these with owners and managers that are thinking about trying Schedulefly - so thanks a bunch y'all! We intended to leave the note up for a few days - but after just 3 hours we had over 200 really amazing responses from all different types of restaurant industry professionals. People from both the front and back of house too. I've added a few below. Here are a few more.

"I love the fact that I can check my schedule from pretty much anywhere! I really like the text message alerts. Schedule fly makes it so easy to pick up or give up shifts! I also like being able to see who I'm working with before I go in! I LOVE SCHEDULEFLY.COM!!!!"
