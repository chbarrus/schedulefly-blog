Want To Know A Ridiculously Simple - and Cheap - Way To Create Podcasts?

May 2010
We started creating podcasts with customers recently. Before we started, I had no idea how to create them. But I of course wanted to keep it simple, and hopefully do it for not much money. I figured if I had a mic and speakerphone, I could get by with capturing the audio. But I assumed I would need some fancy - and expensive - recording software to record the conversations and turn them into files. I was right about the mic and speakerphone - but very wrong about the software. In a matter of minutes, I found and downloaded Audacity for free. Then I bought a $29.99 microphone.
