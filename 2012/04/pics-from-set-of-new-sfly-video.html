Pics from the set of the new Sfly video...

Wednesday, April 4, 2012
Last week we were filming the new video for our home page at Top of the Hill Restaurant & Brewery in Chapel Hill, NC. Here's a few pics from the shoot.





The video will include clips of people that do all sorts of things for the restaurant (owner, GM, server, hostess, bartender) each saying why they like Schedulefly. The idea is make it clear to anybody viewing the video how Schedulefly will make their life easier, no matter what role they play in their restaurant.

We also recently picked the music and we have incredible b-roll from inside the restaurant and in and around Chapel Hill. This is going to be one badass video!
